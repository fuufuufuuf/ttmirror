Open a video URL in Safari and save it to the iPhone photo library.

## Steps

1. Launch **Safari**
2. Wait for Safari to appear
3. Tap the address bar at the top of the screen
4. Type "${VIDEO_URL}"
5. Press **Return**
6. Wait for the video to load
7. Screenshot: "video_loaded"
8. Tap the share button (box with arrow) in Safari's toolbar
9. Wait for the share sheet to appear
10. Tap "Save to Photos" or "Save Video"
11. If "Allow Safari to access your photos" dialog appears:
    1. Tap "Allow Full Access"
12. Screenshot: "video_saved"
